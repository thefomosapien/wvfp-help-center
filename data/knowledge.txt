**In accordance with the By-Laws of West Valley Fast Pitch, Inc., the Board of Directors has approved the follows Rules and Regulations governing the administration of the softball activities of the organization.**

# REGISTRATION

- **REGISTRATION**

- Eligibility to participate in WVFP is restricted to females in the age classifications set forth by the By-Laws.

- WVFP must hold at least three publicly announced registrations over a period of at least two weeks.

- Early registration may be held for girls having played the previous year.  This must be in addition to the minimum three public registration dates.

- Each registrant must provide the following:

- Copy of state or federal birth certificate or church records for the purpose of proving age.

- Signed parental or guardian consent and waiver forms.

- Signed written certificate from examining physician or a signed waiver by a legal parent or guardian.

- The President shall work to ensure that written consent from a legal parent or guardian for each participant is obtained so that the player may participate.  The standard WVFP form must be used.

- Every girl who registers and is eligible must be placed on a team for the current season.

- WVFP must not limit the number of players to be registered in order to restrict the number of teams in any classification within the WVFP.

- After registration, the elected Vice President for each age classification must report to the Board of Directors the number of girls that have registered.  The Board of Directors shall determine the number of teams to be formed in each age classification taking into consideration all registered girls and possible late registrations.

- Registration forms shall be coded to facilitate team formation as outlined in Article 2 below.

- Birth dates should be recorded directly from the Birth Certificate or Church records but the age should be according to the WVFP age as outlined in Section 2.0.1.

- The Board may establish the minimum and maximum number (not to exceed 15) of participants placed on each team.  All girls must be registered prior to participation in any WVFP activity.

- Registration fees shall be established by the Board of Directors.

- Registration fees are set as follows for the 2017 League Season:

*1 player per family: *		*$**90.00*

*2 **+ players in same family:     $10.00 Discount*

	- A girl in a “family “is defined as a sister, step-sister, cousin, or other relation who reside in the same residence during the current WVFP season.

		- WVFP may refund 50% of the registration fee to a girl who leaves WVFP during the season if, in the opinion of the President, there is a legitimate reason for leaving, such as injury, illness or moving out of the area.

		- Regular season registration fees can be waived for players of WVFP elected officials, appointees or volunteers if conditions of participation are met.  The Board of Directors will determine the level of participation waiver, if any.

		- No girl can be excluded from the program due to inability to pay.  As an alternative, when determined by the President, the girl or parent may work off the registration fee by cleaning up after the games, helping with concessions, etc.  Another alternative may be fundraising methods available from various businesses in the area.  Obligations between the President and the parent must be kept confidential and the girl will be assigned to a team in the same manner as any other participant.

		- The information on the registration form is personal and is kept confidential.

- Any late registrant (after team draws) must sign-up individually (except sister) in an effort to keep the teams in balance.

- Girls will be assigned a “point value” to assist in team formation.  Values are assigned by the age classification Vice President and will be based on a point system.  The intent of this system is to form balanced teams based upon player ability and experience.  Player point value may be based on player evaluations by coaches from the previous year as well as parent’s input (as indicated on the registration form).  Player evaluations by coaches may be gathered at the end of each season for this purpose.

Other factors such as coach experience and ability cannot be factored and may result in unbalanced teams.  The following scale is an outline, or suggestion, regarding the Vice President’s specific player knowledge in awarding a point value that best reflects the player’s knowledge and skill in softball:

| Point Value | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | New player to any organized Softball/Baseball Player is thought to have little or no softball knowledge or skill |
| 2 | Returning Softball Player (never an All-Star) Regardless of number of years played, player is thought to be average in softball knowledge or skills developed. |
| 3 | Returning Softball Player (above average) Regardless of number of years played, player is thought to be above average, but not current age All-Star level regarding softball knowledge or skills developed. |
| 4 | Returning Softball Player (All-Star) Regardless of years played, player is thought to be at All-Star level in softball knowledge or skills developed. |

# TEAM FORMATION

- **TEAM FORMATION**

- WVFP will establish the number of teams to be formed in each age classification.  The classifications are as follows: 8U, 10U, 12U, 14U, 18U.  The WVFP age of each girl is her age as of **January 1 of the current year**.

- A girl may move up in age classification, but she may not return to the lower division within the same season.

- The Board of Directors empowers the President to make decisions of classifications of girls with special needs.  Any such approved cases would allow a girl to play below her natural classification.  However, this will disqualify the girl for All-Star play unless approved by the Board of Directors.

- No girl can be barred from participation or otherwise discriminated against by WVFP because of race, religion, color, creed or political affiliation.

- The President shall be in charge of player assignments in the 18U classification unless a Vice President is appointed to the age classification.  The Vice President of each classification shall be in charge of player assignment in their appropriate age classification.

- Player assignment meetings, or Team Draws, may be held jointly or as a sub-meeting of a regularly scheduled Board Meeting.  Those persons invited to attend are limited to Board members and appointed coaches.

- The intent of the rules governing team formations in WVFP is to discourage the “building” or “stacking” of teams or develop powerful teams that would dominate league play (See Addendum II).  Exceptions are as follows:

- *Daughters of the coach **and 1 assistant coach** **will automatically be assigned to th**e coach’s** team.** *

- *Sisters will automatically be assigned to the same team within a classification unless a written request to assign sisters to different teams is made by the parents.*

- *Two friends may sign up together and play on the same team.  However, only one of them can be a returning All-Star, ****and no girl can have more than one friend sign up as a teammate.***

- Prior to the team formation meeting, or team draws, a coach may make automatic assignment along with the coach’s daughter(s) and/or friend(s) according to Section 2.0.7., the exception would be the combination of daughter(s) and/or friend(s), which could unbalance league teams.

- Any adult can be coach, assistant coach or chaperone as long they are approved by the Board and pass a background check.

- For the purpose of team formation, girls who have played high school softball (whether inter-mural or organized team), accelerated-level softball, or collegiate inter-mural softball the previous year will be considered All-Star level.

- Each Vice President must make every effort to re-register last year’s players prior to team formation.  Once teams have been formed, it is the responsibility of the President to ensure equality.

- No division of girls will be made by position, with the exception of pitchers and/or catchers, as deemed necessary by the WVFP Board of Directors.

- After all teams have been formed, no participant may be re-assigned to another team unless approved by the Board of Directors.

# PRACTICE

- **PRACTICE**

- The President shall secure and maintain access to practice areas used by WVFP.

- Practice of any kind may not start before the date set by the WVFP Board of Directors.  Practice is to be limited to no more than **two hours in duration** if practicing twice in the same day. If practice is held once per day, the practice duration is limited to **no more than three hours**. No organized practice is allowed on Sunday.

- There must be a mixture of male and female adults at all practice sessions.  Help may be provided by the Board of Directors.

- No team under these By-Laws shall practice or play between September 1 and March 1 of the following year, using the name, equipment or field without the written permission from the Board of Directors.  If playing within these dates an equipment fee will be assessed per team.

# GAMES

- **SEASON GAMES**

- The President shall secure and maintain access to game areas used by WVFP.

- The President shall exercise authority to provide orderly conduct of the games and spectators in attendance at all games.

- Schedules will allow teams to play a minimum of 12 games and a maximum of 20 games per season in league play in all age classifications.

- Games will be played as scheduled.  Rescheduling of games will be decided by the Board of Directors and must include proper notification to all concerned, including umpires.

- Team standings will be determined by accumulation of points. Points will be accumulated as follows:

- Win = 2 points per team

- Tie = 1 point per team

- Loss = 0 points per team

- A game that is rained out will be considered complete if 75% of the game time has lapsed (i.e. 57/70 minutes) and the home team is either ahead or has completed the bottom half of the inning.  An incomplete game will be COMPLETED at a later date rather than being re-played.

- A tie game called due to weather or darkness will be considered complete provided 75% of the game time has lapsed.  Each team will receive 1 point toward team standings.  Any variation must be approved by the Board of Directors PRIOR to season play.

- If a game is tied at the end of regulation play, one extra inning MAY or MAY NOT be played.  This decision must be made by the Board of Directors prior to season play.  If the score remains tied, each team will be awarded one point toward team standings.  ***201******9****** Update: We will play ONE extra inning using the International Tie Breaker rule for ******12U, 14U, and 18U teams only.  8U and 10U ******teams must end game when time expires by finishing the inning.******  This rule will be carried over to subsequent years unless otherwise noted.***

- Make-up games should be scheduled as soon as possible.  At the end of the season, make-up games that do not affect the first place standings need not be played.  Consideration for All-Star eligibility should be made prior to cancelling make-up games.

# PLAYING RULES

- **PLAYING RULES**

- WVFP will be played under USA Softball rules except as modified or changed in the following rules in this Article 6.

- Regular season games will have a time of 70 minutes (except 8U – see 8.0.10).

- WVFP league softball sizes:

8U & 10U – 			11-inch ball

	All other classifications – 	12-inch ball

- WVFP will establish a time limit for regular season games, and also a 6-run rule or a mercy rule FOR ALL AGE CLASSIFICATIONS.  Umpires and coaches shall be advised of the WVFP game time limits in order to avoid potential confusion and controversy.

	- The home team is the team listed first on the game schedule.

- During the course of the season, the application of this game Rules shall carry over and be applied from game to game.  **A girl can sit only one inning for every four full innings played** (assumes a 12-player roster).  This is for all league games attended.  After the first violation of this rule, the violation will be addressed between the President (or the Board of Directors) and the coach.  Upon the second violation of this rule, a notation will be placed on the official score card and the team violating this rule will forfeit that game and any additional games in which the rule is violated.

- Any girl who fails to attend practices or games may be disciplined by limiting her participation in a game(s), but only after the coach has discussed the situation and proposed discipline with the President and the President has approved the discipline of limiting the girl’s participation in a game(s).

- Each girl on the roster will bat in order throughout the entire game.

- All players must arrive at the game prior to her first time at bat to be eligible to play in that game.  In league games, the 8, 10, 12 and 14U teams need at least 8 players to hold a legitimate game.  The 16 and 18U teams need at least 7 players.  In 8, 10, 12 and 14U games, while teams may play league games with only 8 players, an out will be recorded each time through the batting order for the missing ninth (9th) batter on the roster.  In 18U games, if a team plays with only 7 players, then an out will be recorded for the missing eighth (8th) batter each time that position comes up on the roster.  No other penalty will be applied as a consequence for the missing player.  The missing player (and consequently the recording of the automatic out) can be placed in any position in the batting lineup.  Keep in mind that by placing the missing player at the bottom of the lineup it allows the missing girl to play provided the batting position has not been passed the first time through the lineup.  Once that missing player’s batting position has passed, it cannot be filled during that specific game.

- The umpire may declare a forfeit when the batting order IS NOT submitted properly, to the opposing coach/scorekeeper.  This will happen after failure to present the line-up in a reasonable timeframe.

- **A rostered member who becomes injured and requires medical treatment or hospital care, under a doctor’s orders, must supply the appropriate Vice President a medical release before she can return as an active player.**

- A coach will not log a visit as a result of an injury, or a time out called by the other team.

- WVFP has free substitution and does not use the re-entry rule.  Since WVFP bats the entire roster, there is no substitution rule for International Tiebreakers and/or courtesy runners for the pitcher and catcher (i.e., speed-up rule).

- A simplified box score and summary sheet will be available through WVFP.

- Metal cleats or spikes are not allowed at any WVFP activity.

- All participants must wear a batting helmet when batting during practice, league play, and tournament play.  Helmets are provided by WVFP.

- All participants acting as catcher must wear a catcher’s mask protector, whether in practice or in games.

- All umpires must wear a mask and throat guard while umpiring a WVFP game while behind the plate and on the playing field.

- A participant cannot be on more than one “Fast Pitch” roster at the same time and must be able to participate in at least 75% of the WVFP season’s regularly scheduled games.

- Roving outfielders shall be allowed in all age classifications, all outfielders and the rover must play at least 5 feet behind the baseline until after the pitch is made.

# PITCHING

- **PITCHING**

- During the regular season, a pitcher must follow the following rules:

a.	8U	Coach Pitch (refer to Subsection 7.0.5 below)

b.	10U	12 outs per week (refer to Subsection 7.0.2 & 7.0.6 below)

c.	12U	12 outs per week (refer to Subsection 7.0.2 & 7.0.6 below)

d.	14U	15 outs per week (refer to Subsection 7.0.2 & 7.0.6 below)

e.	18U	18 outs per week (refer to Subsection 7.0.2 & 7.0.6 below)

***7.0.1****** ******A:  202******2******:  We are testing a no pitch count rule for the 202******2****** season.  No age group will follow the pitch count rule****** above******.  It is highly advised that each team develop a minimum of two pitchers.***

- A week consists of two back to back (consecutive) scheduled games.  Example: games 1 and 2, games 3 and 4, games 5 and 6, etc., will consist of one week.  Any games rained out or started but not completed due to weather or darkness will remain in the original week they were scheduled. ***(******see****** 7.0.1 A)***

- A violation of pitching limit is a forfeit for the violating team.  The Vice President of each age classification is responsible to track and enforce this rule.  Board members may assist as needed.  ***(******see****** 7.0.1 A)***

- A play that results in multiple outs will not penalize a pitcher, if the multiple outs cause a participant to exceed the allotted outs for a week.  ***(******see****** 7.0.1 A)***

- Definition of Coach Pitch.  (THE COACH PITCHER IS NOT A DEFENSIVE PLAYER.)  The team that is at bat will have their coach, or an adult assigned by their coach pitch to them when their team is on offense.  The adult pitcher must not be within 30 feet (8U), or in contact with the pitchers plate (10U) to deliver the pitch.  Each batter in 8U will have five pitches or three strikes to hit the ball before being called out on strikes.  If the batter hits a foul ball on their 5th pitch or 3rd strike they will receive another pitch, no one can be called out on a foul ball.  **The adult pitcher cannot give his/her batting team any help while pitching, either physical or verbal.**  This help is the sole the responsibility of the base coaches only.  No base will be awarded for being hit by a pitch. FAILURE TO FOLLOW THIS RULE WILL RESULT IN THE BATTER BEING CALLED OUT AND ALL BASE RUNNERS RETURNING TO THEIR ORIGINAL BASES.

- An incomplete inning counts as a complete inning when a game is called due to expired time.  A full inning played will count as three outs against the pitcher(s) regardless of the number of outs made.  If more than one pitcher is used to complete the inning with outs remaining, the outs remaining are to be given to the pitcher that pitched the most batters.  Example: The first pitcher pitches to 7 batters with one out, and the next pitches to 2 batters with 1 out.  If a game is called before the inning is over the remaining out will go to the pitcher who pitched to the 7 batters.  The extra out cannot cause a pitcher to forfeit the game.  ***(******see****** 7.0.1 A)***

- **It is the sole responsibility of each coach to inform the opposing team of outs a pitcher has left in that week before the game begins.**  It is also the responsibility of each coach to make sure that the pitcher outs recorded against their pitchers during a game are correct on the official score card after each game before the scorekeeper leaves the field.  ***(******see****** 7.0.1 A)***

- On the occasion of the third time in the same inning a league player pitcher records a hit-by-pitch, each having resulted in the batter being awarded a base, the pitcher **shall **be removed from the pitching position for the remainder of that game, and may play positions other than pitcher for the remainder of the game.

- **Protests must be made before the next pitch or, on the last play of the game, before the umpires leave the playing field**.  The coach of the protesting team shall immediately notify the plate umpire that the game is being played under protest.  The plate umpire shall in turn notify the opposing coach and official scorekeeper.  The intent for this protest must also be noted on the official score sheet at the time including status of each runner, if runners are on base.  Once a protest is registered, the game will continue until finished.  If the protest is upheld, the game will then be replayed from the time of the protest forward.  An official written protest must be filed within a reasonable time with the Board President.

# RULES FOR 8U AGE CLASSIFICATION

- **RULES FOR 8U AGE CLASSIFICATION**

- When a ball is hit inside the base path, play continues until the runner touches first base, then the play is over and the ball becomes dead.  Runners off base can proceed to the base they are going, at their own risk, but cannot go further.  If a play is made on a runner where the ball is misplayed, the runner is allowed one base.  Only one overthrow per batted ball is allowed.

- A ball hit to the outfield allows runners to run as far as they can until the ball is returned inside the base path and is in **control** by a defensive player.  The ball then becomes dead and the runner or runners are stopped at the base they are approaching.

- **Runners can only score on a batted ball.**

- Games may end in a tie.  Each team will be awarded a win on the stat sheets.

- Each side will retire after 6 runs or three outs, whichever comes first.

- Batter may not advance to first base on a third strike.

- Runner cannot steal when on base.

- No in-field fly rule.

- An approved baseball, tee ball, or official softball can be used for batting.

- Regular season games will have a time of 55 minutes.

- SAFETY RULE:  A line parallel with the pitcher’s plate that would extend from the first baseline to the third baseline is the reference point for this rule.  No defensive players may position themselves in front of this line until the ball has been released by the pitcher.  Violation of this rule has the following effect:  The offensive team may choose to take the result of the play, or have a no pitch called.

# WVFP CHAMPIONSHIP AND AWARDS

- **WVFP CHAMPIONSHIP AND AWARDS**

- The team with the greatest number of points (refer to Section 5.0.5) for regularly scheduled league games in each age classification shall be the age division champion, except in the 8U age classification.

- If league play ends in a tie for first place, play-off game(s) are authorized at the discretion of the WVFP Board or the head-to-head win/loss record for the season will be compared.

- Awards will be selected and made at the discretion of the WVFP Board.  Awards may include first, second, and third place for each age classification, MVP, Most Inspirational, etc.

# ALL-STARS

- **ALL-STARS**

- A member of the Board of Directors must be present when All-Stars are selected.

- An All-Star Selection Committee shall be formed for each age classification consisting of WVFP President, Vice President of the age classification and head coach from each team.  If the head coach is unavailable one assistant coach can substitute.

- Each All-Star team will have a coach, an assistant coach and a chaperone (If the coaching staff is all male).  These personnel must have participated in at least 50% of regular season games in any capacity.  Each coach should be allowed to choose their staff with the final decision being the responsibility of the All-Star Selection Committee for each specific age classification.

- Each girl selected for All-Stars must have participated in at least 75% of the WVFP season as originally scheduled.  (See Section 6.0.18).

- A girl may be selected for All-Stars in an age classification, which she participated in the regular season, or for a higher age classification.

- Each All-Star team is to consist of 11-14 players, one coach, an assistant coach and one chaperone (8U may have two additional assistant coaches).  A chaperone is only needed if the coaching staff consists of all male participants.

- Care and attention must be given to the method of All-Star selection prior to the beginning of the regular season.  This is to be in writing and accepted by the Board of Directors.

- The Vice President of each age classification must keep a detailed record of selection procedures for future reference and to use if a replacement player is needed.  The appropriate Vice President is required to prepare an official All-Star Roster containing the name, phone number and address of all team members and coaching staff.  A copy of this must be given to the President in conjunction with the USA Softball roster.  A copy of all All-Star rosters should be given to the Board Secretary for historical records, as well.

# STANDARDS OF THE GAME

- **STANDARDS OF THE GAME**

- Seating areas shall be provided for each team, including those not playing, and participants must remain in this area.  The area is reserved only for participating teams and their coaching staff.  This area is called the “Dugout.”

- Spectators should be separated from the dugout by at least 10 feet.  If possible, arrangements should be made for spectators to sit on the sides of the field and behind the backstop.

- Spectators are expected to conduct themselves in accordance with the aims and goals of WVFP.  The removal of offensive persons from a WVFP game for poor attitude or behavior is the responsibility of the WVFP Board member(s) and/or coaches.

- Use of alcohol, tobacco or profanity on or around the playing field during WVFP play or activities is forbidden.

- Admission fees will not be charged at regularly scheduled league games.

- No business of questionable adolescent benefit may have their name, emblem, or slogan appear as part of WVFP’s program as advertisers, promotions or sponsors.

- Board Members and Coaches should impress upon the parents the need for their positive support on all WVFP games.  This is a very important part of the WVFP program and is a good time to have the participants and spectators experience good sportsmanship.

- No league games or practices are allowed on Sunday.

# INSURANCE

- **INSURANCE**

# **ADDENDUM I**** - ****ALL STARS**

**West Valley Fastpitch – Girls Softball **

**All-Star Coach and Player Selection Process**

**All-Star Coach Selection Process**

League Vice Presidents for each group will solicit interest and recommend All-Star coach selections to the Board.  The Board of Directors will approve the All-Star coaches by a majority vote.  Factors that should be considered in determining coaches include, but are not limited to:

- Head coach experience in that specific age classification during the regular league games.

- Coach has demonstrated some level of success during the current season.

- Coach has previous All-Star experience.

- Coach has demonstrated support of league and Board expectations.

- Coach has demonstrated good sportsmanship, ability to work with parents, and positive relations with other coaches.

The All-Star coach is responsible for determining the number of players for his/her team, recommended number is 12-14, as well as the assistant coaches.  These are usual responsibilities for a coach.  **All-Star**** coaches will be announced and posted no later than the end of the third week in May**** ****-OR- *****after****** ******A******ll-Star players are selected*****.**  This same process is followed if a second All-Star team is created in the specific age classification.

**All-Star**** Player Selection Process**

The regular season is intended to have a competitive balance.  All-Stars are intended to provide the league with at least one team in each age classification that can have success against other league’s All-Star teams.  All players who meet league eligibility rules are eligible to be selected as an All-Star.  When multiple teams are selected from the age classification, the intent is to create the first team with those who have demonstrated the highest abilities and production.  The second team in intended to be both competitive and developmental.

- **Coaches will submit All-Star ballots after each and every regular season game.  **Candidates must receive at least two votes to be considered for All-Star selection.

- Coaches will receive a ballot with all candidates who qualify for All-Star selection during a meeting arranged by the respective Vice Presidents of each age classifications.

- Coaches will have the opportunity to discuss the qualifications of each candidate.

- Each coach may vote for up to 9 All-Star candidates.

- The top nine selections are automatically placed on the All-Star team.  The All-Star coach and age group VP selects the remaining three players for the team.  If the coach decides to have more than twelve players, the coach may select any player from the approved candidate list after all All-Star candidates in that age classification are selected.

- When there are two All-Star teams, the top twenty-four players are selected. The Blue Team (A) All-Star coach and age classification Vice Presidents will choose the first twelve players.  The remaining twelve players will be placed on the White Team (B).

- If siblings with different skill levels are selected, parents may ask for both to play on the same team, at the level of the lesser player.  For example, one makes the first team and the other makes the second team, the parents of the candidates may request for both to play on the second team.

- Once selected as All-Stars, players will be announced at Closing Ceremonies. If the candidate is unable to meet the All-Star requirements, for any reason, (i.e., vacation, illness, injury, etc.), another candidate will be selected from the approved list by the All-Star coach to fill the vacancy created.

**ALL-STAR COMMITMENT**

Players selected as All-Stars are committing to a two month (possibly longer) intensive softball schedule.  To help defray the costs incurred by the league for tournament play, All-Star players are required to contribute a fee determined by the league of no less than $150.00 per player.  Additional costs are covered by the league and/or team fundraisers.

Players selected can expect to practice several times per week and to play multiple softball games.  Players unable to meet this type of schedule should not choose to play in All-Stars.  Absences because of conflicts with other activities, including sports, dance, etc., are not acceptable.

# **7  CONFLICT OF INTEREST POLICY**

**7.0.1  **Purpose: The purpose of this conflict-of-interest policy is to protect the Corporation’s interest when it is contemplating entering a transaction or arrangement that might benefit the private interest of an officer or director of the Corporation or might result in a possible excess benefit transaction. This policy is intended to supplement but not replace any applicable state and federal law governing conflict of interest applicable to nonprofit and charitable organizations. This policy is subject to the mandatory provisions of the Utah Revised Nonprofit Corporation Act, U.C.A. 16-6a-101, et seq., as amended, pertaining to conflicts of interest.

**7.0.2  **Definitions.

**7.0.2.1  **Interested Person. Any Director, officer, or member of a committee with Board-delegated powers, who has a direct or indirect financial interest, as defined below, is an interested person.

**7.0.2.2  **Financial Interest. A person has a financial interest if the person has, directly or indirectly, through business, investment, or family:

**7.0.2.2.1  **An ownership or investment interest in any entity with which the Corporation has a transaction or arrangement.

**7.0.2.2.2  **A compensation arrangement with the Corporation or with any entity or individual with which the Organization has a transaction or arrangement.

**7.0.2.2.3  **A potential ownership or investment interest in, or compensation arrangement with, any entity or individual with which the Corporation is negotiating a transaction or arrangement.

**7.0.2.3  **The term “compensation” includes direct and indirect remuneration as well as gifts or favors that are not insubstantial. A financial interest is not necessarily a conflict of interest. Under Section 7.0.2.4 below, a person who has a financial interest may have a conflict of interest only if the Board decides that a conflict of interest exists.

**7.0.3  **Procedures for Dealing with a Conflict of Interest:

**7.0.3.1  **Duty to Disclose: In connection with any actual or possible conflict of interest, an interested person must disclose the existence of the financial interest and be given the opportunity to disclose all material facts to the Directors and members of committees with Board-delegated powers considering the proposed transaction or arrangement.

**7.0.3.2  **Determining Whether a Conflict of Interest Exists. After disclosure of the financial interest and all material facts, and after any discussion with the interested person, he/she shall leave the Board or committee meeting while the determination of a conflict of interest is discussed and voted upon. The remaining Board members shall decide if a conflict of interest exists.

**7.0.3.3  **Procedures for Addressing the Conflict of Interest:

**7.0.3.3.1  **An interested person may make a presentation at the Board or committee meeting, but after the presentation, he/she shall leave the meeting during the discussion of, and the vote on, the transaction or arrangement involving the possible conflict of interest.

**7.0.3.3.2  **The President shall, if appropriate, appoint a disinterested person or committee to investigate alternatives to the proposed transaction or arrangement.

**7.0.3.3.3  **After exercising due diligence, the Board shall determine whether the Corporation can obtain with reasonable efforts a more advantageous transaction or arrangement from a person or entity that would not give rise to a conflict of interest.

**7.0.3.3.4  **If a more advantageous transaction or arrangement is not reasonably possible under circumstances not producing a conflict of interest, the Board shall determine by a majority vote of the disinterested Directors whether the transaction or arrangement is in the Corporation’s best interest, for its own benefit, and whether it is fair and reasonable. In conformity with the above determination, it shall make its decision as to whether to enter into the transaction or arrangement.

**7.0.3.4  **Violations of the Conflict-of-Interest Policy:

**7.0.3.4.1  **If the Board or committee has reasonable cause to believe a member has failed to disclose actual or possible conflicts of interest, it shall inform the member of the basis for such belief and afford the member an opportunity to explain the alleged failure to disclose.

**7.0.3.4.2  **If, after hearing the member's response and after making further investigation as warranted by the circumstances, the Board or committee determines the member has failed to disclose an actual or possible conflict of interest, it shall take appropriate disciplinary and corrective action.

**7.0.3.5  **Records of Proceedings. The minutes of the Board and all committees with Board-delegated powers shall contain:

**7.0.3.5.1  **The names of the persons who disclosed or otherwise were found to have a financial interest in connection with an actual or possible conflict of interest, the nature of the financial interest, any action taken to determine whether a conflict of interest was present, and the Board's decision as to whether a conflict of interest in fact existed.

**7.0.3.5.2  **The names of the persons who were present for discussions and votes relating to the transaction or arrangement, the content of the discussion, including any alternatives to the proposed transaction or arrangement, and a record of any votes taken in connection with the proceedings.

**7.0.4  **Compensation.

**7.0.4.1  **A voting member of the Board who receives compensation, directly or indirectly, from the Corporation for services is precluded from voting on matters pertaining to that member’s compensation.

**7.0.4.2  **A voting member of any committee whose jurisdiction includes compensation matters and who receives compensation, directly or indirectly, from the Corporation for services is precluded from voting on matters pertaining to that member's compensation.

**7.0.4.3  **No voting member of the Board or any committee whose jurisdiction includes compensation matters and who receives compensation, directly or indirectly, from the Corporation, either individually or collectively, is prohibited from providing information to any committee regarding compensation.

# **8  INDEMNIFICATION**

**8.0.1  **Indemnification of Directors, Officers, Etc. The Corporation hereby declares that any person who serves at its request as a director, officer, employee, chairman or member of any committee shall be deemed the Corporation's agent for purposes of this Article and shall be indemnified by the Corporation against expenses (including attorney's fees), judgments, fines, excise taxes and amounts paid in settlement actually and reasonably incurred by such person who was or is a party, or is threatened to be made a party, to any threatened, pending or completed action, lawsuit or proceeding, whether civil, criminal, administrative or investigative, by reason of such service to the Corporation, provided such person acted in good faith and in a manner he or she reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Corporation and, with respect to any criminal action or proceeding, had no reasonable cause to believe his or her conduct was unlawful. The determination of whether a person is entitled to indemnification under this Article shall be made in the specific case based on the circumstances of the case, considering the applicable standard of conduct. Except as provided in Section 8.0.3, termination of any such action, suit or proceeding by judgment, order, settlement, conviction or upon a plea of nolo contendere or its equivalent, shall not, of itself, create either a presumption that such person did not act in good faith and in a manner which he or she reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Corporation or, with respect to any criminal action or proceeding, a presumption that such person had reasonable cause to believe that his or her conduct was unlawful.

**8.0.2  **Indemnification Against Liability to Corporation. No indemnification shall be made in respect of any claim, issue or matter as to which a person covered by Section 8.0.1 shall have been adjudged to be liable to the Corporation, or derived an improper personal benefit, whether or not involving action in his or her official capacity, in which proceeding he or she was adjudged liable on the basis that he or she derived an improper personal benefit, unless and only to that extent that the court in which such action, suit or proceeding was brought shall determine upon application, that, despite the adjudication of liability but in view of all the circumstances of the case, such person is fairly and reasonably entitled to indemnification for such expenses which such court shall deem proper.

**8.0.3  **Indemnification in Criminal Actions. No indemnification shall be made in respect of any criminal action or proceeding as to which a person covered by Section 8.0.1 shall have been adjudged to be guilty unless and only to the extent that the court in which such action or proceeding was brought shall determine upon application that, despite the adjudication of guilt but in view of all the circumstances of the case, such person is entitled to indemnification for such expenses or fines which such court shall deem proper.

**8.0.4  **Other Indemnification. The indemnification provided by this Article shall not be deemed exclusive of any other rights to which any person may be entitled under Utah law, the Utah Revised Nonprofit Corporation Act, U.C.A. 16-6a-101, et seq., as amended, the Corporation’s Articles of Incorporation, any agreement, any other provision of these bylaws, a vote of disinterested directors or otherwise, and any procedure provided for by any of the foregoing, both as to action in his or her official capacity and as to action in another capacity while holding such office.

**8.0.5  **Period of Indemnification. Any indemnification pursuant to this Article shall (a) be applicable to acts or omissions which occurred prior to the adoption of this Article, and (b) continue as to any indemnified party who has ceased to be a director, officer, employee, or agent of the Corporation and shall inure to the benefit of the heirs and personal representatives of such indemnified party. The repeal or amendment of all or any portion of these bylaws which would have the effect of limiting, qualifying or restricting any of the powers or rights of indemnification provided or permitted in this Article shall not, solely by reason of such repeal or amendment, eliminate, restrict or otherwise affect the right or power of the Corporation to indemnify any person, or affect any right of indemnification of such person, with respect to any acts or omissions which occurred prior to such repeal or amendment.

**8.0.6  **Insurance. The Corporation shall purchase and maintain insurance, in such amounts as the board may deem appropriate, to insure the Corporation against any liability, including without limitation, any liability for the indemnifications provided in this Article.

**8.0.7  **Right to Impose Conditions on Indemnification. The Corporation shall have the right to impose, as conditions to any indemnification provided or permitted in this Article, such reasonable requirements and conditions as the Board of Directors may deem appropriate in each specific case, including but not limited to any one or more of the following: (a) that any counsel representing the person to be indemnified in connection with the defense or settlement of any action shall be counsel that is mutually agreeable to the person to be indemnified and to the Corporation; (b) that the Corporation shall have the right, at its option, to assume and control the defense or settlement of any claim or proceeding made, initiated or threatened against the person to be indemnified; and (c) that the Corporation shall be subrogated, to the extent of any payments made by way of indemnification, to all of the indemnified person’s rights of recovery, and that the person to be indemnified shall execute all writings and do everything necessary to assure such rights of subrogation to the Corporation.

=== SOURCE: D1 Prospects Master Rules (2025) — WVFP's tournament sanctioning/officiating partner ===
Note: D1 Prospects governs D1-sanctioned tournaments (like the Gerald Wright Classic and other tournament play).
WVFP also tries to follow these rules informally during regular season league play where practical, but WVFP's
own Rules & Regulations and By-Laws are the primary governing document for regular season play. Where the
two sources disagree, both are noted below — check which context applies (regular season vs. tournament).

2025 Softball Rule Changes
- Rule 1-8-6: High school coaches may use electronic devices in the dugout for one-way communication to the
  catcher while on defense only. Violators are subject to ejection unless the offense is minor.
- Rule 3-6-11: Coaches are prohibited from using the device to communicate with any other team member
  while on defense, or any team member while on offense, and cannot use the device outside the dugout/bench area.
- Rule 9-3-2b: When a base-runner is declared out for being hit by a fair batted ball, the batter-runner is
  credited with a fielder's choice in the scorebook.

2025 Points of Emphasis
- Pace of play: 60 seconds is provided between innings for the defense to take the field/warm up and the
  offense to prepare to bat. After that, the batter has 10 seconds to enter the box (Rule 7-3-1) and the pitcher
  has 20 seconds to deliver a pitch (Rule 6-2-3). Violations result in a Strike or Ball depending on which side
  was tardy. The same 20-second pitcher clock applies on every pitch, not just between innings.
- Situational awareness: once a game has begun, only the batter, runner(s), on-deck batter, coaches in the
  coach's box, bat shagger, and nine defensive players may be outside the dugout/bench or warm-up areas.
  Hitting the ball to teammates on defense during play is prohibited. Between innings, bench personnel may
  throw and run. On-deck batters must use the on-deck circle closest to their own dugout (Rule 7-5-1).

Tournament Registration
- Teams do not check in; everything is completed online via Tourney Machine. Rosters and waivers, with
  parent/guardian emails (not coach emails), must be completed and approved by 5pm two days before the event.
- Team insurance policy must be uploaded to the Tourney Machine account under "Manage Files."
- Coaches confirm by registering that their team is legally equipped and compliant with D1-Prospects/NFHS rules.

Pitching Distance
- 10U: 35 feet
- 12U: 40 feet
- 14/16/18U: 43 feet

Refund / Weather / Cancellation Policy (tournament events)
- If an event is canceled before any games start: 80% refund. If canceled after 1 scheduled game has started:
  50% refund. After 2+ games started: no refund. No refund for a team that forfeits or chooses not to complete
  the event regardless of games played.
- Team-initiated cancellation: 90% refund 30+ days before the event; 75% refund 14-30 days before; no refund
  within 14 days of the event or once the schedule is released (the team stays on the schedule and forfeits games).
- Showcases, college expos, and camps: no refunds past the registration deadline.
- A $50 minimum admin fee (or 20% of entry fee, whichever is greater) applies to registrations/cancellations.
  Credit card/processing fees are never refunded.
- Late fee: $50 if entry fee isn't paid in full by the registration deadline.

Rules and Regulations (D1-Prospects tournaments; based on NFHS Softball Rules with these exceptions)
1. Field of play: unusual conditions (spectators/obstacles too close) may prompt special ground rules from the
   Director/Umpire, but these never supersede the rule book. Curfews are honored and may adjust playing times.
2. Equipment: Bats must not be on the USA/USSSA non-approved list and must carry proper certification marks.
   Illegal bats mean ejection of the player and head coach. **14/16/18U players may wear metal cleats. 12U, 10U,
   and 8U players may NOT wear metal cleats.** (Compare: WVFP's own Rules & Regulations ban metal cleats for
   every age at any WVFP activity — a stricter, blanket rule. For a regular WVFP league game, no metal cleats
   regardless of age. For a D1-sanctioned tournament, older divisions may be allowed metal cleats per D1's rule.)
   Coaches are responsible for checking all players are properly equipped.
3. Start times: teams should be ready 15 minutes before posted start time. Time starts at the coin flip.
4. Home team: decided by coin flip in pool play; in bracket play the higher seed chooses (coin flip if tied
   seeds); in the championship, the undefeated team is home.
5. Coach attire: team uniform/coach shirt or team colors or khaki/black/white/gray; no cut-off jean shorts;
   closed-toed shoes required on the field.
6. Sportsmanship: zero tolerance for disruptive behavior by fans, coaches, or players — taunting, profanity,
   intimidation, demeaning others, charging an umpire, or arguing judgment calls. Penalty is ejection unless
   judged minor (then a warning first). Coaches are responsible for their fans' behavior. Anyone ejected 3 times
   in a rolling 6-month period gets a 6-month suspension at D1's discretion. Spectators may never address
   umpires on or off the field.
7. Lineup cards: must include team name, coach name(s), player first initial + last name, jersey number (no
   duplicates on a team), position, and batting order. Cards become official once exchanged and accepted by
   the plate umpire pre-game. Unauthorized changes trigger a team warning, then ejection of the head coach for
   the rest of the tournament and the player being called out, at the umpire/director's discretion.
8. Re-entry rule: starters and substitutes may leave and re-enter the game once, in the same batting order spot.
9. Batting order: teams may bat their whole lineup or a straight-9 lineup with free defensive substitution in
   all divisions (pool and bracket play alike), as long as offensive integrity is maintained.
10. Courtesy runners: only for the pitcher and/or catcher. If batting the full lineup, any player not on base
    can run; if not batting the full lineup, the courtesy runner must come from the listed substitutes. The
    same runner can't run for both the pitcher and catcher in the same game.
11. Playing short-handed (8U/10U/12U only): a team may play with one batting position vacant if at least 8
    batters are present; an out is declared when that spot comes up. A team dropping below 8 batters forfeits.
    Multiple absent players are allowed under the same conditions. An absent player generally may not return to
    the lineup, with a narrow exception for the Blood Rule.
12. Pitching: leaping is allowed (both feet may leave the ground) as long as the pivot foot stays within/partially
    within the 24-inch pitcher's plate and doesn't replant farther from the plate.
13. Between-innings warm-ups: 60 seconds to throw 5 pitches and be ready to bat.
14. Game length: Pool play is 75 minutes, no new inning (90 minutes at some events), and may end in a tie.
    Bracket play: higher seed's choice, 75 minutes or 7 innings, whichever comes first — must have a winner.
    Championship: undefeated team is home, 7 innings or a time limit by age (90 min for 14U-18U, 80 min for
    12U, 75 min for 10U [60 min at some events], 60 min for 8U).
15. Run rules: game ends if a team is ahead by 12 runs after 3 innings, 10 after 4, or 8 after 5 complete
    innings, in every phase. Pool play only, an additional rule ends the game at 8 runs after 70 minutes (85
    minutes at 90-min events), drop dead. In 8U/10U/12U, each team may score a max of 6 runs per half inning in
    pool play only (an over-the-fence home run can exceed it).
16. Tie games: International Tie Breaker (ITB) starting next inning — last completed batter from the previous
    inning starts on 2nd base; a second ITB round adds a runner on 1st too.
17. Scoring: umpires keep the official on-field score, checking with each team's scorekeeper each half-inning.
    The home team's scorebook is official unless the plate umpire rules otherwise.
18. Players/rosters: teams need a minimum of 8-9 players to start and 8 to continue. Falsifying identity is
    grounds for ejection. Only rostered players (frozen 5pm two days before the event) may play. Player age
    eligibility is based on age as of August 31 of the prior year; a player may always play up, never down, and
    players more than 2 years younger than the division need approval.
19. Protests: only rule application or illegal-player use may be protested — judgment calls cannot be
    protested. A $100 on-the-spot fee is required (refunded if the protest is won). Must be raised with the
    plate umpire before the next pitch (or before umpires leave the field if the game just ended).
